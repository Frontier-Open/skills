# Media policy

## Purpose test

Include an image only when it does at least one of the following:

- proves or documents a factual claim;
- explains a mechanism or comparison;
- shows the actual product or interface being discussed;
- provides a memorable, original editorial metaphor for the thesis.

Decorative stock photography, generic robots, glowing brains, city skylines, and ornamental charts without data do not pass this test.

## Priority

1. Primary-source chart, filing excerpt, paper figure, or official product screenshot.
2. Original explanatory diagram built from verified facts.
3. Brand-led editorial illustration generated specifically for the article.

## Requirements

- Save a local production copy.
- Record alt text, caption, credit, and original source URL.
- Crop without changing the meaning of charts or screenshots.
- Never remove watermarks or attribution.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted editorial photography merely because it is accessible.
- Generated images must not impersonate documentary evidence. Record `generated: true` and `prompt_note`.

## Sizes

- WeChat cover: 900 × 383, 2.35:1, critical content centered for platform crops.
- Web/social OG: 1200 × 630, 1.91:1.
- Inline: prefer WebP or PNG, 1,200–1,600 px wide, optimized without making text unreadable.

Keep cover typography inside the deterministic renderer. When using an editorial illustration, avoid embedding generated text in the image.
